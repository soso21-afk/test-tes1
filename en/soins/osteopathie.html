What is Osteopathy?

Osteopathy is a therapeutic discipline based on manual techniques to diagnose, treat and prevent dysfunctions of the body. It focuses on the musculoskeletal structure and its relationship with the body's other systems, including the nervous and circulatory systems. Osteopaths work with the muscles, bones and joints to improve mobility, relieve pain and support overall health.
