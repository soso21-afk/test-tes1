Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What is naturopathy?

Naturopathy is a holistic approach to health that uses natural methods to support and stimulate the body's ability to heal itself. It encompasses a variety of practices, including nutrition, exercise, stress management and the use of natural remedies.

What is micronutrition?

Micronutrition focuses on providing essential micronutrients — such as vitamins, minerals and trace elements — in precise amounts to optimise health. It aims to correct nutritional deficiencies and improve cellular metabolism.

What issues can naturopathy and micronutrition address?

These approaches can be used to treat and prevent various health issues, such as: digestive disorders (bloating, constipation, etc.), chronic fatigue and low energy, skin problems (eczema, acne), weight management and obesity, food allergies and intolerances, immune-system support, and hormonal imbalances.

What happens during a naturopathy and micronutrition consultation?

A typical consultation starts with a full assessment of your state of health, including a discussion of your diet, lifestyle, medical history and current symptoms. The naturopath may recommend tests to identify nutritional deficiencies. A personalised plan is then created, including dietary advice, nutritional supplements and lifestyle recommendations.

Can naturopathy be combined with other medical treatments?

Yes, naturopathy can be used alongside conventional medical treatments. It is important to inform both your doctor and your naturopath of all the treatments you are following, to ensure effective coordination and avoid interactions between medicines and supplements.

Is naturopathy safe for everyone?

Naturopathy is generally safe for most people, but as with any health approach, it is important to consult a qualified professional. Some natural remedies can interact with medication or may not be suitable for certain health conditions.
