Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What is dietetics?

Dietetics is a therapeutic process involving regular appointments between a patient and a qualified dietitian to address various nutritional and dietary health issues. It helps people understand their eating habits and nutritional needs, and develop strategies to improve their physical well-being and prevent diet-related illness.

What happens during a dietetics session?

A dietetics session is generally a conversation between the dietitian and the patient. The dietitian listens, asks questions and helps you explore your eating habits, nutritional needs and health goals. Sessions may include practical advice, discussion of your medical and dietary history, and the creation of personalised nutrition plans. The length and frequency of sessions vary according to your needs.

How long does dietetic support last?

The duration of dietetic support depends on your goals and the nature of the health issues being addressed. Some people benefit from short-term consultations (a few weeks to a few months) for specific dietary adjustments, while others may need longer-term follow-up (several months to several years) for chronic conditions or long-term health goals.

Is dietetics effective?

Yes — dietetics is an effective way to improve health and overall well-being. It helps manage and prevent various conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, cardiovascular disease and digestive disorders. Dietetics can also play a key role in weight management, better digestion and the development of healthy eating habits.

Is dietetics confidential?

Absolutely. Confidentiality is a fundamental part of dietetic care. Information shared between the patient and the dietitian is protected, except where there is an immediate risk to the patient or to others, in which case steps may be taken to ensure safety.

Is dietetics covered by insurance?

In Switzerland, some basic and supplementary insurance policies cover dietetic consultations, especially when they are carried out by accredited dietitians or under the supervision of a doctor. We recommend checking the specific terms of your insurance policy.
