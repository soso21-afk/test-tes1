Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What is floatation?

Floatation is a deep-relaxation method that takes place in a pool of water saturated with Epsom salt, allowing the body to float effortlessly. The environment creates sensory isolation by removing the sensations of gravity, sound and light, making it easier to reach a state of complete relaxation.

What are the benefits of floatation?

Floatation offers many benefits, including: reduced stress and anxiety, helping to lower cortisol levels and induce deep relaxation; relief from muscle and joint pain thanks to floating in weightlessness; better sleep, making it easier to fall asleep and improving sleep quality; and enhanced creativity and focus, in an environment ideal for reflection and meditation.

What happens during a floatation session?

A floatation session starts with a shower to remove impurities. You then enter a pool of warm water with a high concentration of Epsom salt. The water temperature is set to match your body temperature, removing any sensation of heat or cold. Sessions generally last between 60 and 90 minutes. You can choose to float in complete darkness or with soft lighting, depending on your preference.

Is floatation safe?

Yes, floatation is generally safe for most people. However, it may not be suitable for people with certain skin conditions, recent injuries or severe claustrophobia. We recommend consulting a health professional if you have any particular concerns.

Are there any precautions to take before a floatation session?

It is best not to shave or wax just before a session, as the salt can cause irritation. We also recommend avoiding caffeine or stimulants before the session to make it easier to relax.

How many floatation sessions are recommended?

The recommended number of sessions depends on your personal goals. Some people feel the benefits after a single session, while others benefit from regular sessions for ongoing relaxation and stress management.
