Ask a question

Insurance

Are your treatments reimbursed by insurance?

Several of our treatments are covered depending on your policy: dietetics is reimbursed by basic insurance (LaMal), and many therapies (craniosacral, acupuncture…) are covered by supplementary insurance (ASCA & RME).

Are you ASCA and RME accredited?

Yes, several of our therapists are ASCA and/or RME accredited, which allows reimbursement by most supplementary insurance policies. The details are confirmed when you book your appointment.

How do I get reimbursed?

You pay for the session, then send the invoice to your supplementary insurance. We provide a receipt that meets ASCA / RME requirements.

General questions

Where is the centre located?

In the heart of Rolle, Rte de la Vallée 7, ideally placed between Geneva and Lausanne, with easy access and parking nearby.

How do I book an appointment?

By phone on +41 21 826 00 88, by e-mail, or via the contact form. Our team will guide you towards the treatment best suited to your needs.

Do I need a prescription?

Most of our wellness treatments and therapies are available without a prescription. Some medical care may require one — we will advise you case by case.

Types of treatment

What is the difference between medical care, therapies and wellness?

Medical care is provided by health professionals (dietetics…), therapies bring together manual and natural approaches (osteopathy, acupuncture…), and wellness covers recovery and relaxation experiences (floatation, cryotherapy…).

Are the treatments suitable for everyone?

Most of our treatments are suitable for a wide audience. Some practices have contraindications (pregnancy, specific conditions); our team always discusses them before the session.
