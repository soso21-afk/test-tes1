Regeneratium: your haven of peace and well-being in Rolle

A holistic, personalised approach

Looking for a revitalising escape for body and mind? A stone's throw from Lake Geneva, between Geneva and Lausanne, lies a haven of peace dedicated to well-being: Regeneratium. Born from Loïc and Marco's passion for natural therapies and science, this centre offers a holistic, innovative approach to help you regain your inner balance and optimise your health.

A holistic, personalised approach

Regeneratium stands out for its commitment to complete, tailor-made care. Far from being just another wellness centre, it combines innovative treatments and ancestral therapies to meet each person's specific needs.

Whether you want to...

Relieve chronic pain: osteopathy, craniosacral therapy and acupuncture will bring lasting relief.

Regain energy and vitality: cryotherapy, floatation and naturopathy will help boost your organism.

Relax and let go of stress: massages, halotherapy and floatation will give you a moment of serenity.

Enhance your physical appearance: technological slimming, maderotherapy and facial treatments will highlight your natural beauty. ... Regeneratium puts a team of experienced, passionate professionals at your disposal to accompany you on the path to well-being.

Innovative treatments for deep regeneration

Regeneratium draws on the latest scientific advances to offer cutting-edge treatments:

Cryotherapy: plunge into extreme cold to stimulate blood circulation, reduce inflammation and speed up muscle recovery.

Halotherapy: breathe the pure, salty air of a salt cave to purify your airways and improve your skin.

Floatation: experience weightlessness in a pool of water saturated with Epsom salt, for deep relaxation and an unrivalled feeling of lightness.

T-Shape: reshape your figure and fight cellulite with this technology combining radiofrequency, suction and laser.

A multidisciplinary team at your service

The Regeneratium team is made up of qualified, experienced therapists, each specialised in their own field: osteopaths, naturopaths, beauticians... They work in synergy to offer you comprehensive, personalised care.

Nathalie, Michael, Sarah and Karen, ASCA massage therapists, offer relaxing and therapeutic massages to release tension and encourage deep relaxation.

Osvaldo and Anne-Catherine, DO ASCA osteopaths, help you regain mobility and relieve pain through gentle, precise manipulations.

Gaëlle, ASCA naturopath and micronutritionist, guides you towards a healthy, balanced diet to optimise your health and well-being.

Maria, in charge of aesthetics and reception, welcomes you warmly and advises you on the treatments best suited to your needs.

A commitment to your lasting well-being

Beyond the treatments themselves, Regeneratium is committed to giving you the keys to lasting well-being. The team helps you understand your body and your needs, encourages you to adopt a healthy lifestyle and gives you personalised advice to maintain your balance every day.
